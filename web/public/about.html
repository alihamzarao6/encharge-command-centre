Encharge Capital Command Centre

A private work tool run by Encharge Capital, a Perth finance broker.

What it does

  • Shows the business's leads and where each one is up to.
  • Answers questions about documents and web pages the broker and staff choose.
  • Helps draft writing in the business's own voice.

Who can use it

Only Encharge Capital's broker and staff, with an account Encharge Capital has set up. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up. Staff sign in here.

Google Drive

Staff can connect their Google Drive to ask about a file without downloading it first. The Command Centre can open only the files they pick, and never lists their Drive. The privacy policy and terms of use say exactly what it does with them.
